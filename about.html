About Luxoria

We write about leather the way a craftsman would explain it.

Luxoria is a small, independent journal for people who own a leather bag and would like to understand it. We are interested in materials and care: what a hide is, how it is finished, why it marks and how to treat it kindly.

We do not sell bags or care products. We do not review, rank or authenticate brands, and nobody pays to be mentioned. We make no claims about what any bag is worth.

Every guide is written from handling and caring for leather, compared with what tanneries and professional leather cleaners publish, and reviewed by a second reader. Where home treatment is risky, we say so and recommend a specialist.
